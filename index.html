Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026 | Applies to all users in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile Information: Full name and Gmail address, stored locally on your device only.
  • Order Details: Name, phone number, and delivery address to process your reservation.
  • Device Data: Browser type and session data to improve performance. We do not track your precise location.
  • Images: Reference images sent via private request are transmitted directly to our Telegram concierge.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your orders via Telegram.
  • To contact you regarding delivery status and payment confirmation.
  • To maintain your local order history on your own device.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not operate a central customer database.

4. Telegram Communication

Order details are transmitted via Telegram Bot API. This is subject to Telegram's own Privacy Policy at telegram.org/privacy.

5. Cookies

OUMRAN STORE uses only essential browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights

  • Clear your profile by clearing your browser's local storage.
  • Request deletion of order data by contacting us on Telegram.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions: @OUMER_FETE

Refund & Exchange Policy

Last updated: January 2026 | Applies to all orders in Addis Ababa

Your satisfaction is our priority. All refund and exchange requests must be initiated within the timeframes specified below.

1. Eligibility

  • Item received is significantly different from what was ordered.
  • Item has a clear manufacturing defect or damage upon arrival.
  • Request submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Non-Eligible Items

  • Items worn, washed, or altered after delivery.
  • Minor color variations due to screen differences.
  • Sale or discounted items (unless defective).
  • Custom private sourcing orders — non-refundable once confirmed.

3. How to Request

  • Contact @OUMER_FETE within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Provide your order ID, a clear photo of the item, and a description.
  • Our team reviews within 1–2 business days.

4. Resolution Options

  • Exchange: Delivery of the correct item at no additional cost.
  • Store Credit: Applied toward your next order.
  • Full Refund: Issued via Telebirr within 3–5 business days.

5. Contact

@OUMER_FETE on Telegram.
