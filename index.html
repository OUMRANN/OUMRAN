Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026  |  Applies to all users in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used. By using OUMRAN STORE, you agree to the terms below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile Information: When you create an OUMRAN profile, we collect your full name and Gmail address, stored locally on your device.
  • Order Details: When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, and delivery address to process your reservation.
  • Device Data: We may collect browser type and session data solely to improve store performance. We do not track your precise location.
  • Images: If you submit a private sourcing request with a reference image, that image is transmitted directly to our Telegram concierge and is not stored on any third-party server by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your orders via Telegram.
  • To contact you regarding your delivery status and payment confirmation.
  • To maintain your local order history on your own device.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

All profile and order history data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not operate a central customer database. Your data remains on your device and under your control.

4. Telegram Communication

Order details and private sourcing requests are transmitted via Telegram Bot API to our internal business channel. This communication is subject to Telegram's own Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing it at telegram.org/privacy.

5. Cookies

OUMRAN STORE uses only essential browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) required for core functionality such as cart management and profile saving. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

6. Your Rights

  • You may clear your profile and order history at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.
  • You may request deletion of any order data shared with us by contacting us on Telegram.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, reach us via Telegram: @OUMER_FETE

Refund & Exchange Policy

Last updated: January 2026  |  Applies to all orders in Addis Ababa

Your satisfaction is our priority. Please read this policy carefully before placing your order. All refund and exchange requests must be initiated within the timeframes specified below.

1. Eligibility for Refund or Exchange

You may be eligible for a refund or exchange if:

  • The item received is significantly different from what was ordered (wrong color, wrong size, wrong product).
  • The item has a clear manufacturing defect or damage upon arrival.
  • Your request is submitted within 48 hours of delivery.

2. Non-Eligible Items

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered after delivery.
  • Items where the issue is a minor color variation due to screen display differences.
  • Orders where the correct size was confirmed at checkout and delivered as specified.
  • Sale or discounted items (unless defective).
  • Custom or private sourcing orders — these are procured exclusively for you and are non-refundable once confirmed.

3. How to Request a Refund or Exchange

  • Contact us via Telegram at @OUMER_FETE within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Provide your order ID (found in your Order History), a clear photo of the item, and a description of the issue.
  • Our team will review your request within 1–2 business days.

4. Resolution Options

  • Exchange: We will arrange delivery of the correct item at no additional cost.
  • Store Credit: Applied toward your next order.
  • Full Refund: Issued via Telebirr to your registered number if an exchange is not possible. Processed within 3–5 business days.

5. Upfront Payment Refunds

If you paid a 50% deposit or full upfront payment and your order cannot be fulfilled (due to stock issues), a full refund will be processed to your Telebirr account within 3–5 business days.

6. Contact

All refund and exchange communications should be directed to: @OUMER_FETE on Telegram.
